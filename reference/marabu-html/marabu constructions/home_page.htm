Stage 01 · Setting Out

Every project begins as geometry

Whether it's a villa, a bridge or a fabrication shed — grids strung, benchmarks fixed, levels transferred to ±0.00 before anything moves.

Stage 02 · Foundations

Engineered to the soil, not to habit

Pad footings, piles or raft — designed from the soil report and cast on blinding. The invisible storey, checked hardest.

no structure outruns its foundation

Stage 03 · The Skeleton

Bones of concrete & steel

Columns rise, slabs land on bolted bearing plates — concrete frame or structural steel, the same discipline either way.

Stage 04 · Level on Level

A rhythm you can trust

Floor follows floor, decks span, bracing holds everything true while the structure finds its strength — villa or viaduct alike.

Stage 05 · Topping Out

The last beam is gold

The final member goes up painted gold — the ironworkers' tradition we honour on every Marabu site, from shed roof to bridge deck.

topped out — safely, together

Stage 06 · Core & Circulation

A building is also a route

Lifts, stairs and ramps thread the frame — circulation planned as carefully as structure, accessible end to end.

Stage 07 · Closing It In

Roof on, weather out

Gable, skylight or curved shell — the envelope goes on detailed to the millimetre, dry below from the first sheet.

Stage 08 · Fit-Out

Interiors take shape

Kitchens, joinery, false ceilings, cladding — our interior studio fits the building around real lives, not the other way round.

Stage 09 · The Details

The part everyone remembers

Railings, steps, terraces, planting, the last coat of finish — small lines that separate a build from a home.

measure twice — admire forever

Stage 10 · Services & Load Path

Breath and bones

Ducts, conduits, fire lines — and the load path from roof to footing marked, checked and signed in gold.

Stage 11 · Life at 1.8 m

The only load that matters

People move through at human scale — families in villas, crowds on bridges, teams in workspaces. The building finally becomes itself.

Stage 12 · Handover

Keys, warranty, pride

Snags closed to zero, documents bound, warranty handed over — every trade, one signature. That's the Marabu sequence.
